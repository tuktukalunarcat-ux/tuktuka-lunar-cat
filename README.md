## 🐾 About the song

"Киска, крыска" is a playful romantic comedy in song form.

The relationship constantly moves between:

- teasing
- biting
- kissing
- jealousy
- affection
- pretending to leave
- immediately coming back

The recurring "мяу-мяу" motif became the central hook.

The musical direction combines:

- Russian dance-pop
- funky bass
- disco guitar
- bright synthesizers
- hand claps
- modern pop drums
- male lead vocals
- female response vocals
- spoken comedy
